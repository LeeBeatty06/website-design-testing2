5 Minute Practice

Focus on the present rather than the final aesthetic result

  1. Select an object around you
  2. Set a timer for 5 minutes (or whatever is most comfortable)
  3. Place your pen on the paper and look at your object
  4. Draw the details on your object without lifting the pen or looking at your paper. Look down once the timer goes off or when you feel like you're done.

After looking at your work, feel free to color over it and add any details!
